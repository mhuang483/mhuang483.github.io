Classic Zelda - Unity; C# Recreation

Team project to recreate the first dungeon of The Legend of Zelda (1986). I created the weapons (sword, arrow, boomerang, and bomb) , the management of the player such as health and damage, player and enemy knockbacks, and room controlling (freezing and spawning enemies). Included in the game is a custom level with a custom weapon. Press '4' (non number pad) to play the custom level!

Progress Through Death - Unity; C# Creation

Individual project made over two weeks. In this game, death is not the end! The corpses of your past may help you reach the goal! Progress though each level to reach the victory screen! Alternatively, each challenge level leads to the victory screen!
